Privacy and safety

Your notes stay
with your device.

Not Getting Married is designed offline first. This page explains how the app handles data and the limits of its content.

Data handlingSystem permissionsExports and backupsDisclaimerYour control

Data handling

Life notes, plans, contacts, saved replies, and settings you create in the app are stored on your device by default. The app does not require an account and does not upload this content to developer-operated servers.

Deleting the app, losing the device, or failing to keep a backup can remove local data. Use the encrypted backup tool when needed and keep the password and file somewhere safe.

System permissions

Privacy lock uses your device passcode or Face ID, verified by iOS. Reminders run only after you enable them and grant notification permission. Contacts, calling, sharing, and files are passed to system services only when you choose those actions.

Exports and backups

Encrypted backups are created on the device. The app does not store the password, and a forgotten password cannot be recovered. Exported PDFs are not encrypted and may include the contacts, plans, and notes you select. Share them only with people you trust.

You are responsible for your backup files. Before deleting local data, confirm that a backup is saved and can be restored with the correct password.

Disclaimer

Content in the app supports communication preparation, life organization, and personal notes. It is not a psychological test or diagnosis and is not medical, legal, financial, insurance, or safety advice.

Templates, examples, and replies are suggestions. You decide whether and how they fit your situation. For health, legal, financial, or immediate safety decisions, seek an appropriate professional or local emergency service.

You enter and verify emergency information yourself. The app does not contact other people, replace a caregiver, or provide rescue services.

Your control

You can view, edit, export, or delete your records in the app. Deleting local data requires confirmation. Copies exported to Files or other locations are managed and deleted by you in those locations.
