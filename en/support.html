Not Getting Married runs fully offline. These answers cover the most common questions about using, backing up, and restoring the app.

Getting started

What should I do the first time I open the app?

Read and accept the disclaimer, then start using the app. You can write one thing you handled today, open life plans, or go straight to marriage-pressure replies.

How do I change the tone of a reply?

Open Replies from the bottom tab, choose Random, Gentle, Brief, Firm, Humorous, or Change topic, then tap Another reply.

Backups and restore

Where are backup files?

Open Me → Backup, restore, and PDF. Encrypted backups are listed in the app’s local backup area and can also be shared to Files or another location.

What if I forget my backup password?

There is no password recovery for encrypted backups. Keep the password safe and verify a backup before deleting local data.

What does restore replace?

Restore replaces local plans, contacts, saved replies, settings, and existing records. Current data is not changed until the file and password pass validation.

Privacy lock

Privacy lock uses your device passcode or Face ID. Enable a device passcode in iOS settings and allow device authentication.

Language, appearance, and currency

In Me → Display, choose Follow System, English, or Chinese. You can also choose light or dark appearance and a default currency. Changes apply immediately; existing amounts are not converted.

Data and storage

The app has no account or server. Records stay on your device. If saving fails, check available storage. Uninstalling removes local records that have not been exported, so verify a backup first.
